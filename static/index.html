Cryptographic Architecture & Security Boundary

NIST STANDARDS

1. High-Sensitivity Route (AES-256-GCM):

  • Uses NIST SP 800-38D compliant Galois/Counter Mode with 256-bit key.
  • Generates a fresh cryptographically secure 96-bit (12-byte) nonce for every encryption operation. Nonce reuse is strictly guarded.
  • Computes a 128-bit authentication tag ensuring confidentiality and tamper resistance.
2. Experimental Lightweight XOR Route Limitations (FR-06):
XOR encryption is evaluated strictly as an experimental lightweight research comparison for resource-constrained IoT devices. Bitwise XOR stream operations do not provide production-grade healthcare confidentiality. Under keystream reuse, plaintext recovery attacks are trivial. HMAC-SHA-256 provides integrity authentication only and does not fix XOR's inherent confidentiality limitations.

3. Key Storage & Privacy Policy (NFR-02):

  • Keys are never embedded in frontend code, DOM assets, or exported reports.
  • Development keys are generated ephemerally in memory at startup.
  • All telemetry is synthetic; no patient data leaves the local workstation.
